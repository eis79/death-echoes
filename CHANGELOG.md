# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Death leaves behind a persistent, translucent, glowing echo that replays your last 10 seconds alive, on a loop, forever.
- Right-click your own echo to reclaim 50% of the experience you lost on death.
- Up to 3 echoes per player at once — the oldest fades when a 4th death occurs.
- Echoes render with the owner's skin, ambient soul particles, and an occasional eerie sound.
- Safe echo placement — never spawns inside lava or the void.
- Works in every dimension.
- Fabric (requires Fabric API) and NeoForge builds for Minecraft 26.1.2+.
